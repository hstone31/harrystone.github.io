Evapotranspiration & Eddy Covariance

[Term, Institution] · In development

How do we measure the water an ecosystem gives back to the atmosphere? This course walks through evapotranspiration and latent heat flux from first principles — turbulence, covariance, and the surface energy balance — and has students compute fluxes themselves from real high-frequency data collected at the US-Slt (Silas Little) flux tower in the New Jersey Pine Barrens.
