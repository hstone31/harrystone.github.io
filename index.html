About

I study how water moves between soils, plants, and the atmosphere. My work combines eddy covariance flux measurements with high-resolution remote sensing to understand how ecosystems use water — and where that water comes from — especially during dry periods.

Current projects use repeat drone-based LiDAR to map plant growth across a mountain watershed in Colorado and relate it to soil moisture, groundwater, and terrain. I also work with flux tower data to teach how evapotranspiration is measured and calculated.

Interests

  • Ecohydrology and evapotranspiration
  • Eddy covariance and surface energy balance
  • Drone LiDAR and canopy structure
  • Plant water use and groundwater–vegetation interactions
